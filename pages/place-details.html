Petra (البترا)

The Rose-Red City of the Nabataeans

Location: Wadi Musa, Ma'an Governorate, Southern Jordan

Carved directly into vibrant rose-colored sandstone cliff faces over two millennia ago, Petra served as the thriving trade capital of the Nabataean Kingdom, linking ancient caravan routes between Arabia, Egypt, and the Mediterranean.

Makan Flyover ✈

See Petra from an aerial perspective before stepping foot on its ancient trails.

Key Points of Interest (POIs)

  • The Siq: A dramatic 1.2-kilometer natural gorge flanked by towering 80-meter cliffs.
  • The Treasury (Al-Khazneh): Elaborate classical facade carved into the rock face.
  • The Street of Facades & Theater: Nabataean tombs and a massive Roman-expanded amphitheater.
  • The Royal Tombs: Monumental burial facades facing the sunrise across the valley.
  • The Monastery (Ad-Deir): A monumental plateau temple reached via an 800-step cliff stairway.
