About Ahmad

Born and raised in the heart of Wadi Rum's protected red sands, Ahmad belongs to the Zalabiya Bedouin community. For over twelve years, he has guided travelers across historical desert trails, sharing ancient navigational techniques by the stars, desert foraging knowledge, and fireside oral traditions passed down through generations.
