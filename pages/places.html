Historic Landscapes & Living Destinations

Every corner of Jordan holds distinct geological marvels, ancient architectural heritage, and hospitable communities waiting to be explored beyond standard travel routes.

Featured Jordanian Places

  • Petra

    Nabataean Capital & Rose Sandstone City

    Location: Ma'an Governorate, Southern Jordan

    Carved directly into red sandstone cliffs over two millennia ago, Petra was the bustling trade hub of the Nabataean civilization.

    Explore Petra (Includes Aerial Flyover)

  • Wadi Rum

    The Valley of the Moon & Bedouin Living Heritage

    Location: Aqaba Governorate, Southern Jordan

    Monumental sandstone massifs rising from open valleys, preserved and navigated by local Bedouin tribes for centuries.

    Explore Wadi Rum

  • Jerash (Gerasa)

    Greco-Roman Colonnades & Decapolis History

    Location: Jerash Governorate, Northern Jordan

    One of the best-preserved Roman provincial towns in the world, featuring paved colonnaded streets, theaters, and hilltop temples.

    Explore Jerash

  • Ajloun

    Pine Highlands, Medieval Castles & Ancient Olive Groves

    Location: Ajloun Governorate, Northern Jordan

    Famous for its 12th-century Ayyubid castle, Mediterranean pine forests, and centuries-old olive farming communities.

    Explore Ajloun
