There's more to a place than a destination.

Makan helps people discover places through local experiences, people, stories, and immersive exploration.

We believe travel shouldn't be about ticking boxes off a checklist. A place is not just geographic coordinates, stone monuments, or scenic landscapes—it is defined by the communities that inhabit it, the generational traditions they preserve, and the stories they share.

The Five Pillars of Makan

  • 📍 Places

    Documenting destinations authentically from the red sands of Wadi Rum to the historical alleyways of As-Salt.

  • ✨ Experiences

    Engaging hands-on cultural activities led by authentic local community members, not commercial tours.

  • 👤 People

    Connecting curious travelers directly with artisans, guides, and storytellers who bring each destination to life.

  • 📖 Stories

    Editorial archiving of living heritage, traditional crafts, culinary oral histories, and cultural preservation.

  • 🎥 Immersive Exploration

    Visual previews and aerial flyovers enabling explorers to understand geographic context before stepping foot there.

From Jordan to the World

Makan begins in Jordan 🇯🇴—a land rich in hospitality, ancient civilizations, and diverse regional micro-cultures. Our vision expands from Jordan across the Middle East, setting a new benchmark for documentary, community-first tourism platforms worldwide.

What We Stand For

  • Authenticity over Staging: We refuse generic stock aesthetics in favor of documentary reality.
  • Community Empowerment: Direct economic benefit and platform ownership for local hosts.
  • Cultural Preservation: Archiving traditions, craft wisdom, and local heritage with dignity.

Become Part of the Journey

Whether you want to explore the unseen corners of Jordan or share your craft with mindful explorers, there is a place for you.
